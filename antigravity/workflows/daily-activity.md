description: Generates a summary of activity for a specified date (defaulting to today).
---

1. View the **daily-activity** skill at `antigravity/skills/daily-activity/SKILL.md`.
2. Follow the instructions in the skill file. 
   - If the user provided a date (e.g., `/summary 2024-01-20` or `/summary yesterday`), follow the "Custom" date instructions in the skill.
   - Otherwise, follow the "Default" (Today) instructions.
